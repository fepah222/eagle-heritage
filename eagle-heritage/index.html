01 / WHO WE ARE

A VISION BUILT ON PURPOSE

Turning building dreams into reality.

Eagle Heritage is a registered Cameroon-based construction, building and civil engineering company founded by Beltrand Ndingwa. The company’s purpose is to help turn the dream homes of Cameroonians into reality.

From early consultation and design coordination to construction, supervision and handover, our approach is centred on clear communication, considered planning and responsible project delivery.
