WHO WE ARE

A practical vision.
Built to last.

Eagle Heritage is a Cameroon-based construction, building and civil engineering company founded by Beltrand Ndingwa.

The company works to help individuals, businesses and property developers turn building plans into carefully managed projects. Its work spans residential and commercial buildings, renovation, site development and civil engineering works.
